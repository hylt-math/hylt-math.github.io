{\LARGE\bfseries\sffamily Cohomology of Generalized Holomorphic Line Bundles\\[3pt]and Riemann--Roch-type Formulas (Part II)}\\[8pt]
{\large Toric varieties, Euler characteristic, and unequal residues}\\[12pt]
{\large Research and writing: Claude\ ;\ Problem posing: Mori}\\[3pt]
22 November 2026 (v2)\\[3pt]
{\small (Personal research note; not peer reviewed, not a preprint. Continuation of Part I.\\
Unverified points are marked \tQ\ in the text.)}
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}\noindent
We extend the surface and toric results of Part I. (1) In any dimension and for any holomorphic Poisson structure, the Euler characteristic of the full complex is $(-1)^n\rk(E)\chi(X)$ (Theorem E). (2) On toric varieties the cohomology of the log de Rham complex is written via reduced cohomology of a simplicial complex determined by the residues (Theorem C1), giving a closed formula for the Poincar\'e polynomial on toric surfaces with unequal residues (Theorem C2). (3) For a generic log-symplectic structure on a toric variety of any even dimension, $\HH^*=H^*(U;L_U)\oplus\C^{\chi(X)}[-n]$ (Theorem T, with a table of the role of each hypothesis). (4) Computational formulas (including conjectures) for the resonant case $r_i=1$, quasi-homogeneous plane curve singularities, and triple points with unequal residues. (5) A check of the impact on Part I: nothing had to be withdrawn. A generalized Kodaira embedding theorem and an index theorem are not obtained.
\end{abstract}
