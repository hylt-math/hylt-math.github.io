\begin{center}
{\LARGE\bfseries\sffamily Cohomology of generalized holomorphic line bundles (Part IV)}\\[8pt]
{\large Regular type (Gauss--Manin) versus log-symplectic type,\\ and a reduction theorem in general dimension}\\[12pt]
{\large Research and writing: Claude\ \slash\ Problem posing: Mori}\\[3pt]
22 November 2026 (v1)\\[3pt]
{\small (Personal research note; not peer reviewed, not a preprint. Continuation of Parts I--III.\\
Unverified points are marked \tQ\ in the text.)}
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}\noindent
Parts I--III treated the type-jumping (log-symplectic) case. Here we (1) write the generalized Dolbeault cohomology in the regular-type case (flat symplectic fibre bundles $Y\to M\to X$) in terms of Gauss--Manin flat bundles (Theorem \ref{thm:L}: $\HH^k=\bigoplus_{a+b+j=k}H^a(X,\wedge^bT_X\otimes V^j\otimes G)$, via the Leray double complex and Deligne's Lefschetz lemma); (2) deduce a degree-wise Riemann--Roch formula, polynomial in $m$ (Corollary \ref{cor:L}), and its relation to the Serre-type vanishing of Pal--Poddar (Thm.\ 11.12); (3) contrast it numerically with the type-jumping case as ``$d_1=[\sigma,\cdot]$ almost exact or not'', i.e.\ ``polynomial vs periodic''; (4) in general dimension for log-symplectic structures, reduce the Part II conjecture to acyclicity of the quotient complexes on each stratum (GEN$'$) (Theorem \ref{thm:R}: under (GEN$'$), $\HH^*\cong H^*(U;L_U)\oplus\C^{\#D_{(n)}}[-n]$). Sufficient conditions for (GEN$'$) in general and a unified framework for the two settings (Conjecture \ref{conj:U}) remain open.
\end{abstract}
