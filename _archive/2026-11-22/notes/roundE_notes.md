# Round E — generalized Kodaira embedding: formulation and simplest cases (2026-11-22)
Legend: [P] proved here (hand proof, standard facts named) · [C] cited · [V] verified by script · [?] conjectural.
Scripts (cp2/): roundE_veronese_poisson.py, roundE_correct.py, roundE_numeric.py, roundE_verify_float.py.
Setting: (X,σ) compact complex manifold with holomorphic Poisson σ, J_σ the β-transformed GC structure, A=(T*_X)_σ, GH line bundle L = Poisson module = flat A-connection D (D_α s, α∈Ω¹: O-linear in α, D_α(fs)=fD_αs+σ(α,df)s, flat). HH^0(L) = {s∈H^0(X,L): D_α s=0 ∀α} = "GH sections" = ker(d_1: H^0(L)→H^0(T⊗L)).

## 1. What "embedding by GH sections" can and cannot mean

### Prop 1 [P] (GH sections are locally constant where σ is non-degenerate)
Hyp.: X connected, σ non-degenerate on a dense open U (σ^n≢0), L a GH line bundle. Then dim HH^0(L) ≤ 1; any GH section is determined by its value at one point of U; in particular the "Kodaira map" by GH sections maps X to a point (P^0) or is undefined.
Proof. On U, σ^♯:T*→T is an isomorphism of Lie algebroids A|_U ≅ T_U, so D becomes an ordinary flat holomorphic connection ∇ on L|_U (∇_v s := D_{(σ^♯)^{-1}v}s). A GH section satisfies ∇s=0 on U. U is connected (complement of a proper analytic subset of connected X), L has rank 1, so flat sections form a ≤1-dimensional space, s|_U is determined by s(x_0), and s is determined by s|_U (identity theorem). ∎
Role of hypotheses: connectedness and density of U ⇒ identity theorem; non-degeneracy ⇒ A→T iso ⇒ D is a connection in the ordinary sense. [V] matches tables: P² with Poisson cubic: HH^0(O(m)) = ℂ·F^k for m=3k≥0, 0 otherwise; P¹×P¹ with smooth (2,2) curve: ℂ·F^{m/2} for m∈2ℤ_{≥0} (cp2_notes, p1p1 tables). Hence **no Kodaira embedding by GH sections exists for any m in these examples** (the map is constant).

### Prop 2 [P] (regular type k<n: GH sections are constant along leaves)
Hyp.: M compact, J regular of type k<n on an open dense W (leaves of dimension 2(n−k)>0), L a GH line bundle. Then for every GH section s and every leaf N⊂W the section s is parallel for the leafwise flat connection induced by the L-connection; hence for GH sections s_0..s_N the map φ=[s_0:...:s_N] (defined off the base locus) is constant on each leaf of W: φ is not an immersion on W.
Proof. In the regular chart (Darboux, Gualtieri 2011 Thm 4.3 [C]) π_T(L) ⊃ T_F⊗ℂ (product model: L=graph(ω_Y)⊕(T^{0,1}⊕T*^{1,0}), π_T(L)=T_Y⊗ℂ⊕T^{0,1}). The L-connection satisfies D_ξ(fs)=π_T(ξ)(f)s+fD_ξ s, so D_ξ s=0 ∀ξ∈L gives ∇^F s=0 for the leafwise connection ∇^F. Along a path in a leaf, parallel transport P: L_x→L_y satisfies s(y)=Ps(x) for every GH section, so the ratios [s_0:…:s_N] coincide at x and y. ∎
Example [P] (product M=Y×X, L=pr_Y^*F⊠pr_X^*G): HH^0(L)=H^0_dR(Y,F)⊗H^0(X,G); H^0_dR(Y,F) is ≤1-dimensional per component of Y (flat sections of a line bundle), so φ factors through pr_X (or X×π_0(Y)). Consistent with Künneth in serre_rr_statements.md.
Conclusion: a literal "M ↪ P(HH^0(L)^*)" Kodaira theorem is false for every GC structure of non-complex generic type; the correct generalizations must be formulated on the leaf space (§2a) or by embedding the *Poisson pair* (§2b).

## 2. Alternative formulations

### 2a. Leaf-space Kodaira theorem (regular type) [P, easy]
Theorem K1. Hyp. (K1) π:M→X proper submersion (or orbifold submersion) with connected fibres = the leaves of the symplectic foliation, X a compact complex manifold (orbifold) of dimension k; (K2) L is an SGH line bundle on M whose leafwise holonomy (flat connection on each fibre) is trivial, equivalently L≅π^*L_X as SGH bundles for a holomorphic line bundle L_X→X; (K3) L_X is ample (positive).
Conclusion: ∃ m_0 s.t. for m≥m_0 HH^0(M,L^m)=π^*H^0(X,L_X^m), and the GH-section map φ_m: M→P^N is φ_m=ι_m∘π where ι_m:X↪P^N is the classical Kodaira embedding (for X orbifold: ι_m embeds the coarse space after m divisible by the isotropy orders, Baily 1957 [C]). Hence φ_m separates leaves and has fibres exactly the leaves.
Proof. HH^0 = sections flat along leaves and holomorphic transversally. By (K1),(K2) a section of L^m restricted to a fibre N_x is a flat section of the trivialized flat bundle, i.e. a constant, so sections of L^m = π^*(sections of L_X^m) with transversal holomorphicity = holomorphic sections of L_X^m. Classical Kodaira (Griffiths–Harris Ch.1 §4; Huybrechts Prop 5.3.1 [C]) gives (K3) ⇒ embedding. ∎
Where hypotheses enter: connected compact fibres ⇒ flat sections on a fibre are constants (and fibres of φ_m are whole leaves); trivial holonomy ⇒ L has any GH section on the fibre (if the holonomy of L|_{N_x} is non-trivial, HH^0 vanishes along N_x); positivity of L_X ⇒ Kodaira on X; orbifold ⇒ Baily. Relation to Pal–Poddar: their positive SGH line bundle (Defs 11.10–11.11, unverified here) is of this type; Thm 11.12 is the vanishing counterpart. I did not find K1 stated in the literature; it is a direct reduction, not a deep result.

### 2b. Poisson–Kodaira: embedding the Poisson pair
Idea: since GH sections are ≈ constants, ask instead that the Kodaira embedding φ_m=|L^m|: X↪P^N (classical, X projective, L ample) be a Poisson embedding for some Poisson structure σ_N on P^N, i.e. σ is the restriction of σ_N and X is a Poisson subvariety. For quadratic σ_N (homogeneous quadratic Poisson on ℂ^{N+1}) the O(m) are Poisson modules, matching the module structure of L^m.

Lemma 3 [P modulo a standard check] (Poisson modules of rank one = C^*-invariant lifts). For L a Poisson module, the total space C=L^*∖0 carries a unique C^*-invariant Poisson bivector π̂=σ+uθ∧∂_u (locally, frame e, D_αe=θ(α)e, u fibre coordinate), p_*π̂=σ, and sections of L^k are the degree-k homogeneous functions. Jacobi for π̂ ⇔ flatness+Leibniz for D. Example: P², Π_F^{ij}=ε_{ijk}∂_kF quadratic on ℂ³, L=O(m) all m.
Prop 4 [P] (graded Poisson algebra). L ample Poisson module on projective X ⇒ R=⊕_{k≥0}H^0(L^k) is a graded Poisson algebra with bracket of degree 0 ({R_a,R_b}⊂R_{a+b}).
Prop 5 [P + C] (almost-Poisson quadratic lift). For m≥m_1, R^{(m)}=⊕_kR_{mk}=S/I with S=Sym R_m generated in degree 1 and I generated by quadrics (classical: Mumford, Varieties defined by quadratic equations, 1970 [C]; Serre–Castelnuovo regularity). Then there is a skew biderivation {,}' of degree 0 on S (a quadratic almost-Poisson structure on ℂ^{N+1}) inducing the bracket of R on S/I.
Proof. Choose a linear section R_{2m}→S_2 of the surjection S_2→R_{2m}; lift {y_a,y_b}:=s({ȳ_a,ȳ_b}) and extend by Leibniz. For q∈I_2, g∈S_1: {q,g}' ≡ {q̄,ḡ}=0 mod I_3, so {I_2,S_1}'⊂I_3; I generated by I_2 ⇒ {I,S}'⊂I by Leibniz. ∎
Its Jacobiator J∈Hom(Λ³S_1,I_3); different lifts differ by ε∈Hom(Λ²S_1,I_2). Poisson extension ⇔ J(Π_0+ε)=J_0+L(ε)+J(ε)=0 has a solution ε (a quadratic system); the first-order obstruction class is J_0 mod im L.

Theorem PK-toric [P] (Poisson–Kodaira for torus-invariant σ). X smooth projective toric (torus T=N⊗ℂ^*), σ=Σω^{ij}ξ_i∧ξ_j, ξ_i=x_i∂_{x_i}, ω∈∧²N_ℂ (a regular holomorphic Poisson structure on X), L=O(D) ample with polytope P, m≥1 with L^m very ample (sections χ^a, a∈mP∩M). Then ν:X↪P^N, ν^*y_a=χ^a, is a Poisson embedding for the *diagonal quadratic Poisson structure* σ_N=Σ_{a<b}ω(a,b)y_ay_b∂_{y_a}∧∂_{y_b} on P^N, where ω(a,b)=Σω^{ij}a_ib_j. In particular every very ample power works; no obstruction.
Proof. (1) σ_N is Poisson: for skew λ_{ij}, {y_i,y_j}=λ_{ij}y_iy_j satisfies Jacobi, since the cyclic sum of {y_i,{y_j,y_k}}=λ_{jk}(λ_{ij}+λ_{ik})y_iy_jy_k vanishes by skew-symmetry (six terms cancel in pairs); it is weight-0 for the scaling, hence descends to P^N. (2) On the torus, {χ^a,χ^b}=σ(dχ^a,dχ^b)=ω(a,b)χ^{a+b}=ν^*(ω(a,b)y_ay_b) since χ^aχ^b=χ^{a+b}. So ν is Poisson on T, hence on its closure X (both σ and ν^*σ_N holomorphic). ∎
Hypotheses and roles: toric+torus-invariance ⇒ brackets of characters are monomials ⇒ the lift can be chosen diagonal with no choice of splitting (this is exactly the freedom that makes the generic lift of Prop 5 fail Jacobi); very ampleness ⇒ ν is an embedding.
Cross-check [V]: for σ=Π_{xyz} on P² (toric), the *natural* lift (Prop 5 with lexicographic splitting) at m=2 has Jacobiator ≠ 0 (1 triple, 2 terms), but Jacobi corrections exist (below), consistent with PK-toric.

### Computations for non-toric (P², σ=Π_F, F cubic), Veronese m=2 (ℂ^6, I_2 of dimension 6, 90 unknowns ε) [V]
Natural lift: Jacobiator nonzero for F=Fermat (18 triples), Hesse (19), xyz (1), cusp. Solving the full quadratic Jacobi system J_0+Lε+J(ε)=0 by Levenberg–Marquardt (940 equations, 90 unknowns, random starts): exact-looking solutions (residual ≤1e-12) found for F = xyz, Fermat, Hesse (x³+y³+z³−21xyz), cusp (x³−y²z). For F=Fermat, independently re-verified with finite differences: max|Jacobiator|~1e-9 at random points (float noise), restriction error to the Veronese cone ~1e-10 (roundE_verify_float.py). Note for Fermat the *linear* part is inconsistent (no ε with J_0+Lε=0), so the quadratic term J(ε) is essential. Hence the Veronese-2 embedding of (P²,Π_F) is a Poisson embedding into a quadratic Poisson structure on P^5 in all four cases tested (m=1 is tautological; m≥3 not computed: 1215 unknowns, too heavy for this method).
Status: numerical existence (not an exact algebraic certificate; the reduced residual is machine-precision, and the toric case is proved by PK-toric).

### Conjecture PK (Poisson–Kodaira) [?]
For (X,σ) projective holomorphic Poisson and L ample Poisson module, ∃ m_0 such that for m≥m_0 the Kodaira embedding by |L^m| is a Poisson embedding into (P^N,σ_N) for some quadratic Poisson structure σ_N. Evidence: PK-toric [P]; P² with four cubics at m=2 [V]. Open: general obstruction theory (J_0 mod im L is a Poisson-cohomology-type class: expect vanishing for m≫0 by Serre-type vanishing of the relevant H^1 of a Koszul complex — not proved); does *every* σ on every projective X admit an extension? (no counterexample found, none expected for m≫0; possible failure for small m).
Relation to leaf-space Kodaira: if σ_N=0 on a Poisson subvariety structure, i.e. σ symplectic, Poisson embedding means symplectic leaf is immersed; for σ=0 PK reduces to classical Kodaira; for regular type with M→X (symplectic Y fibres) the Kodaira map factors through X (Prop 2) so the Poisson pair (M, σ_Y) is *not* embedded by sections of leaf-constant L — one needs sections of the *underlying holomorphic* (non-GH) bundle, which is what PK uses.
