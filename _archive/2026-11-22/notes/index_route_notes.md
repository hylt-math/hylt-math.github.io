## 1. Literature survey (what exists, what it does NOT give)

Classifies Hausdorff symplectic groupoids of log symplectic manifolds via the groupoid integrating vector fields tangent to a smooth hypersurface. → the real Lie algebroid T(−log D) ≅ (T*(log D))_{Im σ} is integrable in the log-symplectic case [C, for smooth D; NCD case: abstract speaks of log symplectic manifolds in general, not checked]. Gives the groupoid input for any groupoid-index route.
2. Lanius, *Poisson cohomology of a class of log symplectic manifolds*, arXiv:1605.03854 [C]. Thm 1.5: Poisson cohomology = ^bH^p(M) ⊕ ⊕ H^{p−m}(intersections of hypersurfaces; normal-bundle twists). Real/C^∞ Poisson cohomology, i.e. coefficient O=trivial module, smooth setting; analogous structure ("b-part + boundary terms") to our P_r(t) at r=0 but NOT the same object (we compute holomorphic Poisson hypercohomology). Analogy only.
3. Braverman–Loizides–Song, *Geometric quantization of b-symplectic manifolds*, arXiv:1910.10016 [C]. Role of the line bundle: ∇^L has a log|x| term; no residue is used; modular weights enter only equivariantly. → Strong hint: Euler-characteristic-type indices on the log tangent algebroid are given by Atiyah–Singer integrals over ^bTM with no eta correction — consistent with our finding that the Euler characteristic is χ(X)=∫c_n(T(−log D))-type and blind to r. ## 2. Is the jump at integer residues an indicial-root phenomenon? [D]
Local model near a smooth point of D (coordinate z, L has residue r): the log de Rham complex of the Poisson module acts on z^n (n≥0) by ∇ z^n = (n−r) z^n dz/z (ncd_theorem Lemma 5 stalk computation). Therefore:
- indicial roots in the weight n are exactly n=r, existing iff r∈ℤ; for r∉ℤ the weight-graded complex is acyclic in every weight (⇒ Rj_*=j_!, P_r=χ(X)t²);
- at r=n∈ℤ the weight-n piece survives and is Gr^W of log forms: H^*(D)⊗(1⊕t) with differential given by the Euler class of the normal bundle (Gysin), i.e. **H^*(∂U)** (∂U=boundary of a tubular neighbourhood; circle bundle over D for smooth D: smooth cubic ⇒ 1+2t+2t²+t³). [D: sketch; Gysin identification not scripted.]
- It enters only for n≤0 (Rj_* side: polynomial coefficients n≥0 include n=r) or n≥2 (j_! side: compact support/ideal), and *not* at r=1 for Hodge–Tate X (K_X). The asymmetry r↔2−r is Serre duality (Prop 15).
- χ(∂U)=0 ⇒ the boundary contribution is invisible to the Euler characteristic, exactly as in the BLS b-index (Z-term vanishes in the limit). So: the Euler characteristic ↔ Atiyah–Singer on ^bT; the residue-dependent refinement ↔ kernel of the indicial family = H^*(∂U), the "spectral flow / indicial root" data. This is the APS/Melrose picture in precise outline, **qualitatively** consistent; an actual quantitative APS formula is NOT derived.
- The relation Δ ≠ P_∂ (see §3): e.g. smooth cubic Δ=1−t², P_∂=1+2t+2t²+t³; Δ is the part of the indicial kernel that survives globally: for smooth D, H^*_c(U)→H^*(U) vanishes in degree 2 (dims) and H^*(U)≅ im(H^*(U)→H^*(∂U))=1+2t². [D from the long exact sequence for the smooth cubic; ε no general proof].

## 3. Numerical test of a refined RR "jump formula" [V]
Observed in all exact tables (8 examples, 196 values of m, 0 mismatches; script cp2/index_route_check.py), with r=−m/k, X surface, D nodal, c=#components, ρ=rank⟨[D_i]⟩ in H²(X;C), b1=c−ρ:
  P_r(t) = χ(X) t² + 1_{r∈ℤ≤0}·Δ(t) + 1_{r∈ℤ≥2}·t⁴Δ(1/t),   Δ(t)=1+b1 t+(b1−1)t² = (1+t)(1+(b1−1)t),  r=1 (Hodge–Tate): P=χ(X)t².
Remarks: (i) μ (number of nodes) and the Betti number b2 have disappeared — all is expressed by χ(X) and b1; (ii) Δ(−1)=0 (Euler characteristic untouched); (iii) the jump polynomial depends only on the number of linear relations among the classes [D_i] (b1=dim ker(ℂ^c→H²(X))); (iv) duality P_{2−r}(t)=t⁴P_r(1/t) is manifest.

## 4. Conjecture I3 (index-theoretic refined Riemann–Roch for log-symplectic surfaces) [?]
Let (X,σ,D) as in Thm B, L a Poisson module with constant residue r (all components). Then
 (a) [Euler part, Atiyah–Singer on the log tangent algebroid]  Σ(−1)^k dim HH^k(L) = ∫_X c_2(T(−log D)) + μ = χ(X) — no boundary term, for every r.
 (b) [Indicial part] Define J(r)=P_r(t)−χ(X)t². Then J(r)≠0 only for r∈ℤ∖{1}, J(r)=Δ(t) for r≤0, J(r)=t⁴Δ(1/t) for r≥2, where Δ(t) is a Poincaré polynomial of a sub-quotient of the indicial kernel H^*(∂U) ≅ Gysin(H^*(D)⊗(1+t)) fixed by the spectral-sequence/long-exact-sequence (weight-filtration) comparison H_c^*(U)→H^*(U)→H^*(∂U).
 (c) General dimension and non-NCD D: J(r) governed by spectral numbers (local terms, cp2_second_notes §2–3), anomalies at resonance values.
Not proved: (b) is a fit; the identification of Δ with a sub-quotient of H^*(∂U) is not carried out.

## 5. Obstructions / what a proof would need
- A Fredholm/index theory on a **complex** Lie algebroid L (no J): Hu/PPT apply to real algebroids with J or to groupoid convolution algebras; Morita route via Bailey–Gualtieri (weakly holomorphic symplectic groupoid) not worked out.
- Unimodularity: PPT trace needs an invariant density; for a Poisson structure this is the vanishing of the modular class. [?] For log-symplectic σ the modular vector field w.r.t. a log-volume form is likely zero (σ^n ∈ H^0(−K) log-symplectic gives a canonical log volume), but I did not verify.
- The complex d_L is not elliptic in the usual sense at D (anchor drops rank) but its symbol in the algebroid sense is invertible (Prop 13 of ncd_theorem checks symbol directly); Fredholmness on the open part requires the indicial operators to be invertible ⇔ r∉ℤ: matches the observation that for r∈ℤ the HH groups are the kernels/jumps and for r∉ℤ cohomology is a stable topological Euler-char-only object. [D]
- Honest status: nothing here is an index theorem; it is a roadmap: (1) Fredholm ⇔ r∉ℤ (indicial roots), index = χ(X) (Atiyah–Singer on ^bT, BLS-style); (2) at r∈ℤ the cokernel/kernel jump = H^*(∂U)-type APS correction; (3) a finer graded statement (P_r(t)) needs a *graded* APS/spectral-flow input, for which I found no literature.
