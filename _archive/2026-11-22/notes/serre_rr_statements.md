## A. Serre-type vanishing

### Classical dictionary (what plays which role)
| classical Serre (F coherent, O(1) ample, X projective) | generalized version here |
|---|---|
| coherent sheaf F | the terms K^b = ∧^bT_X⊗E of the Lie-algebroid complex (coherent: A-complex terms are ∧^b of the *module* N* = T_X, E a GH bundle) |
| ample L | holomorphic ample L_0 that is a GH line bundle (Poisson module); the GH structure is only needed to make C(A;·) a complex, not for positivity |
| projective | X projective (existence of L_0 ample; X = complex manifold underlying the β-transform) |
| H^q(F(m))=0, q>0 | E_1^{a,b}=H^a(X,∧^bT⊗E⊗L_0^m)=0 for **a>0** (transversal/Dolbeault degree) |
| consequence: H^*(F(m)) concentrated in degree 0 | HH^* = cohomology of the *finite-dimensional complex of global sections* (Γ(∧^•T⊗E⊗L_0^m), d) |

### Theorem A1 (Serre-type theorem for β-transformed GC structures) [P, any dimension]
Hypotheses: (H1) X compact complex *projective* manifold, dim_C X = n; (H2) σ ∈ H^0(X,∧²T_X) with [σ,σ]=0 (no non-degeneracy, no log condition needed); (H3) E a holomorphic vector bundle with a flat A-connection (GH bundle for J_σ); (H4) L_0 an ample holomorphic line bundle with a flat A-connection (Poisson module).
Conclusion: there is m_0 (depending on X,σ,E,L_0 only through the finitely many coherent sheaves ∧^bT⊗E, b=0..n, and L_0) such that for all m ≥ m_0
 (a) H^a(X,∧^bT⊗E⊗L_0^m)=0 for all a>0 and all b;
 (b) HH^k(E⊗L_0^m) ≅ H^k(Γ(X,∧^•T⊗E⊗L_0^m), d_σ+∇) (cohomology of the global-section complex), in particular HH^k=0 for k>n;
 (c) HH^0 = flat (Casimir) holomorphic sections of E⊗L_0^m.
Proof: L_0^m inherits the tensor-product A-module structure (Leibniz). Serre's theorem (Hartshorne III.5.2) applied to the n+1 coherent sheaves ∧^bT⊗E gives (a). Each K^b is Γ-acyclic, so for the bounded complex K^•=C(A;E⊗L_0^m) of sheaves of C-vector spaces (differentials are C-linear, not O-linear) RΓ(K^•)=Γ(K^•) (acyclic-resolution lemma / the stupid-filtration spectral sequence E_1^{b,a}=H^a(K^b) is concentrated on a=0). This gives (b); k>n vanishing because b ≤ n. (c) is H^0 of the Γ-complex. ∎
Where each hypothesis acts: (H1)/(H4): existence of ample L_0 and Serre's theorem; coherence: finiteness of the family {∧^bT⊗E} (a uniform m_0); (H3),(H4): C(A;·) is a complex and L_0^m is again a module — **nothing else**; non-degeneracy/log condition of σ is irrelevant for A1. Without (H1) (X non-projective, no ample bundle) A1 can fail to have content (no L_0).
Remarks.
 1. Total-degree vanishing is false, already for σ=0 (complex structure): HH^b ⊇ H^0(∧^bT⊗L^m)≠0 for b=0..n. What survives is vanishing in the transversal degree a>0 and in total degree >n. [V]: σ=0 control table e.g. m=0: [1,8,10,0,0] on P².
 2. Effective thresholds by Bott/Künneth [V, serre_rr_check.py]: P² (T(m)=Ω¹(m+3), ∧²T(m)=O(m+3)): E_1^{a>0}=0 iff m ≥ −2 (m=−3: the E_1-terms (2,0),(1,1),(0,2) are all non-zero, total degree 2); P¹×P¹ with L_0=O(1,1): iff m ≥ −1 (m=−2 = K_X exceptional). In both examples the thresholds coincide with the range where the computed tables vanish in degrees ≥3 (all 8 examples, m≥m_0: True). So A1 improves the assumption "U affine" in ncd_theorem Thm 2(v): for ample L_0, vanishing of HH^k, k≥3, holds on surfaces for all m≥m_0 with no affineness hypothesis.
 3. Fails for non-ample twists (e.g. m<0: H_c-type classes in degrees 3,4: [0,0,2,0,1] at m≤−6, 3|m on P²).

### Theorem A2 (periodic stabilisation, log-symplectic surfaces) [P modulo ncd_theorem.md gaps G1,G2,G4]
Hypotheses: X compact complex surface, σ ∈ H^0(−K_X) log-symplectic (zero divisor D reduced, only ordinary nodes, μ nodes), L_0 a GH line bundle with c_1(L_0)=−Σ a_i... precisely residues ρ_i=−a_i, a_i∈Q_{>0} constant on branches (ncd Thm 1, Lemma 4); X simply connected (or π_1(U) abelianisation generated by meridians); X projective & L_0 ample (use A1).
Conclusion: for m ≥ max(1,m_0): HH^k(L_0^m)=0 for k≥3 and HH^*(L_0^m) = H^*(U;𝓛^{(m)}) ⊕ ℂ^μ[−2], where 𝓛^{(m)} has monodromy exp(−2πi m a_i) around D_i; hence depends only on m mod N, N=lcm of denominators of a_i. (Examples: P², m≥1, period 3; P¹×P¹, L_0=O(1,1), period 2.) [V] period k for m≥1 in all 8 computed examples.
Role of hypotheses: R1 residues ≠1 ⇒ the quotient complex Q acyclic except for ℂ^μ[−2] at nodes (ncd Lemma 6); R2 no residue in ℤ_{>0} (m≥1 ⇒ r_i=−m a_i<0) ⇒ log complex ≃ Rj_*𝓛 (Deligne, Lemma 5); ampleness/affineness (A1 or Artin vanishing for affine U) ⇒ degrees ≥3 vanish; simple connectivity ⇒ the local system depends only on m mod N.

### Conjecture A3 (general principle) [?]
For a compact GC manifold (J) and a GH line bundle L_0 that is "positive" in a sense to be defined (candidate: ample on the leaf space of the regular part, plus positivity of the residue along type-change loci), HH^*(L_0^m)=0 in total degree > the maximal b-degree n_max of the Lie-algebroid filtration and the groups are periodic in m. No definition of "coherent filtration with E_1 terms coherent" exists for GC structures that are not β/B-transforms of complex structures. This is the main obstruction to a theorem beyond Thm A1 (type: B/β-transforms of projective complex manifolds) and the regular case.

### Comparison with Pal–Poddar Thm 11.12 (regular type, leaf space Kähler orbifold, SGH line bundles) [C+?]
Product check (derived): M=Y×X, Y symplectic compact, X projective, E=pr_Y^*F⊠pr_X^*G, F flat on Y, G=G_0^m with G_0 ample. Künneth (the L-complex is the tensor product of the de Rham complex of Y and C(T^{0,1}⋉(T^{1,0})*,G) of X): HH^k = ⊕_{j+b+a=k} H^j_dR(Y,F)⊗H^a(X,∧^bT_X⊗G_0^m). By classical Serre (coherent sheaves ∧^bT_X) the terms with a>0 vanish for m≥m_0; the survivors are H^j_dR(Y,F)⊗H^0(X,∧^bT_X⊗G_0^m), generally non-zero in total degrees up to dim Y+dim_C X. So *any* Serre-type vanishing for L=pr_X^*G_0^m holds only in the **transversal Dolbeault degree a**, not in total degree. This is consistent with Thm A1 (same mechanism), with the "transversal formulation" of ncd Thm 2(v), and shows how Thm 11.12(b) must be read if its cohomology contains the leafwise de Rham factor. [G: I did not re-read PP's definition of the cohomology groups and of "q" in 11.12; consistency is therefore a conclusion about the *product case*, not a verification of their statement; **check against arXiv:2404.18113 §11 before citing**.]
Differences: PP require regular type + orbifold leaf space + SGH; A1 requires neither regularity nor orbifold leaf space (type-changing β-transforms allowed, e.g. type 0 off D, type 2 on D) but needs a complex projective underlying X (so only B/β-transforms of complex structures); A2 gives the finer periodic structure in the log-symplectic type-changing case, outside PP's scope (type jumps).

## B. Riemann–Roch-type statement

### What is not enough
Total Euler characteristic χ(HH^*(E)) = (−1)^n rk(E)·χ(X) for every GH bundle (derived for complex structures [P, notes]; for the log-symplectic surface case = χ(U)+μ=χ(X) [P, ncd (iii)]) — blind to c_1(E) and to the residues. Hu's groupoid index is a different number (hu_notes.md).

### Refined invariant: the Poincaré polynomial P_E(t)=Σ_k dim HH^k(E) t^k, governed by the residue r.
**Theorem B (surfaces; = ncd Thm 2 in closed form)** [P modulo G1,G2,G4 of ncd_theorem.md; V 80/80 values + 196 earlier]
Let X, σ log-symplectic with nodal reduced D (c components, rank ρ of the span of [D_i] in H²(X;ℂ), μ nodes), L the GH line bundle with *equal* residue r≠1 on all components (so c_1(L)=r·K_X, L=K_X^{r} in the sense of Poisson modules when r∈ℤ; r=−m/k for O(m) on P² (k=3), O(m,m) on P¹×P¹ (k=2)). Put χ_U=χ(X)−μ, b_1=c−ρ, b_2=χ_U−1+b_1. Then
 P_L(t) = 1 + b_1 t + (b_2+μ) t²          if r∈ℤ_{≤0}   (Rj_*, h^0=1: the Casimir F^{−r});
 P_L(t) = (b_2+μ) t² + b_1 t³ + t⁴        if r∈ℤ_{≥2}   (j_!);
 P_L(t) = χ(X) t²                         if r∉ℤ  (needs H^1(U;L_χ)=0 [G2]);
 P_{K_X}(t) = χ(X) t² if r=1 and h^{p,q}(X)=0 for p≠q (Prop 8).
Consequences: (i) P_L(−1)=χ(X) for all r (Riemann–Roch value); (ii) duality P_{2−r}(t)=t⁴P_r(1/t) [V: 0 violations on a grid of 73 rational r] (formula-level; duality of HH itself is [G9], conjecturally via the Evens–Lu–Weinstein modular-class pairing [C: Quart. J. Math. 50 (1999) 417; applicability unchecked]); (iii) for L=L_0^m, m≥1 this is a periodic function of m (A2), whereas for the **same family** the quasi-polynomial one expects from Hirzebruch–Riemann–Roch is constant: the "Riemann–Roch polynomial in m" is the constant χ(X), and all m-dependence is in the periodic refinement.
Refined numerical invariants that *do* see c_1(L): h^0(L) = 1 iff L≅K^r (r∈ℤ_{≤0}) with canonical module structure (number of Casimirs), h^1−h^0 =b_1−1 on the Rj_* side, and the "boundary defect" dim HH^*(Rj_*) − dim HH^*(j_!) governed by H^*(∂U;L) (below).
Resonances: r=1 (K_X); non-nodal singularities of D at spectral-number resonances (cp2_second_notes §3) [?]; non-reduced D [?]; unequal residues untested numerically [G8].

### What an index-theoretic proof would need [?]
1. *Weighted b-calculus picture*: T_X(−log D) is the b-tangent bundle of the real oriented blow-up; the log complex DR_log(L) is the de Rham complex of this Lie algebroid with flat coefficients. The residue r acts as a **weight** in weighted L² / b-Sobolev spaces; the Rj_*/j_! dichotomy for r∈ℤ_{≤0}/ℤ_{≥2} and the non-integer case (both equal) is the phenomenon that the cohomology of the weighted complex jumps when the weight crosses an indicial root (here the integers). This is exactly the APS/Melrose picture [C: Melrose, *The Atiyah–Patodi–Singer index theorem*, A K Peters 1993; Hausel–Hunsicker–Mazzeo, Duke Math. J. 122 (2004) (weighted L² cohomology; applicability unchecked)]. The long exact sequence … → H^k_c(U;L) → H^k(U;L) → H^k(∂U;L) → … measures the defect by the cohomology of the boundary torus bundle; χ(∂U)=0 explains why Euler characteristics cannot see it. *Conjecture*: dim HH^*(r) is the cohomology of the weighted b-de Rham complex with weight ↔ r, and a refined RR is an APS-type formula whose boundary term is an eta-/spectral-flow-type contribution depending on r mod ℤ and on the position of r relative to ℤ.
2. *Groupoid input*: the Lie algebroid T_X(−log D) ≅ (T*_X(log D))_σ of a log-symplectic structure integrates to a symplectic groupoid [C, from memory: Gualtieri–Li, IMRN 2014; Bailey–Gualtieri arXiv:1611.03850 for the Morita-type statement for J_σ]. Needed: index theorem for the *complex* algebroid L=T^{0,1}⋉A^{1,0} or its Morita equivalent real model; Hu's theorem (hu_notes.md) does not apply to ∧L*⊗E (no J, different complex). Possible route: Connes / Pflaum–Posthuma–Tang index on the groupoid of the real algebroid with a cocycle built from σ (modular class / Ω; for log-symplectic σ the modular vector field w.r.t. ω^n/n! is zero?: **unchecked**).
3. *Local terms at non-normal-crossing points*: spectral numbers of the singularity (cp2_second_notes §2–3) must enter the boundary contribution, hence a refined RR for general (non-NCD) type change requires a theory of weights on non-b-type singular strata [?].
