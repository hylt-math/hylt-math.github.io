## 1. Local stalk theorem for quasi-homogeneous plane curve germs (cusp, tacnode, D_k, E_k, ...)
Setting: h in C{z,w} reduced, quasi-homogeneous, weights (wz,ww), degree d, isolated singularity, Spec = Steenbrink spectrum
(alpha = (wz(i+1)+ww(j+1))/d over a monomial basis z^i w^j of the Milnor algebra O/J), lct=min Spec=(wz+ww)/d (if <=1), delta=d-wz-ww, delta/d=1-lct.
C_s : O --d0--> O^2 --d1--> O  (sigma=h dz^dw, module with parameter s; global O(m): s=-m/3 on P^2, -m/2 on P1xP1), s != 1:
 d0 f=(h f_w+s f h_w) d_z+(-h f_z-s f h_z) d_w ,  d1(p d_z+q d_w)=h(p_z+q_w)-(1-s)(h_z p+h_w q).
### (a) [P] The quotient part is always T[-2], T=O/J, dim T = mu.
 Let B_s = (O -> Theta(-log D) -> hO) (Theta(-log D)={X: X(h) in (h)}). d0 f has X(h)= h(f_w h_z-f_z h_w)+ s f(h_w h_z-h_z h_w) in (h) and d1 maps Theta(-log D) into (h):
 B_s is a subcomplex. Q:=C_s/B_s = [Theta/Theta(-log D) -> O/h], with Theta/Theta(-log D) ~ J.O_D via X -> X(h) mod h (kernel = Theta(-log D); image (J+(h))/(h), and h in J for quasi-homogeneous h),
 and d_Q = -(1-s) * (inclusion J.O_D -> O_D) mod h, injective for s != 1. Hence H^0(Q)=H^1(Q)=0, H^2(Q)=O_D/J.O_D = O/J, dim = mu.
 Long exact sequence: H^1(Q)=0 and H^3(B)=0 => 0 -> H^2(B_s) -> H^2(C_s) -> O/J -> 0 and H^k(B_s)=H^k(C_s) (k<=1). So  stalk(C_s) = H^*(B_s) + mu t^2  (dimension-wise), no connecting-map issue.
 [V] roundF_local_B.py: H(C_s)=H(B_s)+T[-2] for A1,A2,A3,D4,E6 (s in (1/6)Z or finer, 0 mismatches). T dims: cusp 2, tacnode 3 (Tjurina=Milnor).
### (b) [P modulo a sign check] B_s ~ (Omega^.(log D), d + s dlog h ^). sigma^# is an iso Omega^1(log D)->Theta(-log D), Omega^2(log D)=O dz^dw/h -> hO d_z^d_w (plane curves are free divisors, Saito), and sigma^# is a chain map (Lichnerowicz-Koszul-Brylinski);
 the twisting s corresponds to conjugation by h^{s}. The sign/normalisation of s was fixed by the data (weight concentration at k0=-s d, below).
### (c) [P modulo (b)] Weight concentration. e=wz z d_z+ww w d_w in Theta(-log D), e(h)=d h. Cartan: L_e^nabla = nabla iota_e + iota_e nabla and iota_e preserves log forms, L_e^nabla acts on weight-k forms by k+s d.
 Hence H^*(B_s) is concentrated in the single weight k0=-s d (finite-dimensional), and vanishes if k0 < lowest weight of B_s, which is -delta (generated by e in degree 1 and h in degree 2). So H^*(B_s)=0 for s > delta/d = 1-lct. 
 [V] weights of B_s-cohomology printed in roundF_B_*.out: always exactly -s d (cusp s=-5..1/6, tacnode, D4 (s=1/3 at k=-1), E6 (s=1/6 at k=-2)).
### (d) Corrected unified rule (replaces the old [s<lct] rule of Round C, which fails for lct<1/2)  [V] + [C]
 stalk = C^mu[-2] + R(s), R(s) = [s in Z, s<=0](1+t) + (t+t^2) * #{alpha in Spec : s+alpha in Z, s+alpha <= 1},  s != 1.
 The old rule agrees when lct>=1/2 (for s<lct integers n=s+alpha>=2 are impossible) but is wrong for lct<1/2: z^4-w^5 (lct=9/20), s=11/20=1-lct has a class (weight -delta) although s>lct.
 [V] roundF_local_rule2.py: A1, A2 (cusp), A3 (tacnode), D4, D5 (weights (3,2,8)), E6, T236 (z^3-w^6), z^4-w^4, z^4-w^5, z^5-w^5 : 0 mismatches on grids of 1/12..1/20 over s in [-2,2] (s=1 excluded; infinite-dim cases skipped).
 [C] Interpretation: Wang sequence of the Milnor fibration h:B\D->C* (fibre F ~ bouquet of mu circles, monodromy eigenvalues exp(-2 pi i alpha) on H^1(F), trivial on H^0) gives stalk(Rj_* L_s)=[s in Z](1+t)+(t+t^2)#{alpha: s+alpha in Z};
 the logarithmic complex B_s computes the part with weight >= -delta; Calderon-Moreno–Narvaez-Macarro (Proc. London Math. Soc. 98 (2009), DOI 10.1112/plms/pdn043; arXiv math/0603003; pages 要確認) prove LCT for E(kD), k>>0 (Rj_*) and E(-kD) (j_!) for free divisors with Jacobian ideal of linear type (locally quasi-homogeneous ok) — i.e. the same family B_{s} for s shifted by large integers.
 The cut s+alpha<=1 (n<=1) is the weight bound k0>=-delta rewritten alpha-by-alpha; not proved alpha by alpha [?].
 So: PROVED: (a), (c) [given (b)]; and R(s)=0 for s>1-lct. NOT PROVED: that H(B_s)_{k0} has exactly the Wang dimension for s<=1-lct [?, 0 mismatches in 10 types].

## 3. Non-toric dimension 4: products
**Proposition K (Kunneth) [P, modulo Kunneth for hypercohomology 要確認 reference]**: X,Y compact complex manifolds, sigma_X,sigma_Y holomorphic Poisson, L_X,L_Y Poisson modules. For sigma=sigma_X+sigma_Y on X x Y and L=L_X ⊠ L_Y (module structure V_X+V_Y):
 K_{XxY} = K_X ⊠ K_Y as complexes of sheaves ([sigma_X+sigma_Y,.] = [sigma_X,.]+[sigma_Y,.] since [sigma_X,sigma_Y]=0; Koszul sign), hence RGamma(XxY;K)=RGamma(X;K_X)⊗RGamma(Y;K_Y) (Kunneth for sheaf hypercohomology with field coefficients on compact spaces, finite-dim cohomology; e.g. Kashiwara–Schapira, Sheaves on Manifolds, §2 / Bredon, Sheaf Theory, II.15 [C 要確認]) and
 P_{HH}^{XxY}(t)=P^X(t)P^Y(t). [V] consistency: P^2xP^2 toric block product [1,4,12,16,16]=(1+2t+4t^2)^2 (higher_dim_notes.md §4).
 Non-toric instance: X=Y=P^2 with smooth cubic D_X,D_Y, L=O(m)⊠O(m'): HH = product of the Part I tables, e.g. m,m' in 3Z_{>=0}: (1+2t^2)^2 = 1+4t^2+4t^4 = P(H^*(U;C)) with U=U_X x U_Y; m,m' not in 3Z: (3t^2)(3t^2)=9t^4 = P(H^*(U;L_U))+0; m,m' in 3Z_{<=-6}: (2t^2+t^4)^2 = H_c^*(U) [Kunneth for H_c]. D=D_X x Y u X x D_Y is SNC with 2 components, no 4-fold points, so Conjecture C of Round D (generic sigma) predicts exactly H^*(U;L_U) (+0): [P] C holds on these non-toric products (smooth D_X,D_Y).
 Where the factors have nodes: P=(H_X+mu_X t^2)(H_Y+mu_Y t^2) = H_X H_Y + t^2(mu_X H_Y+mu_Y H_X)+mu_X mu_Y t^4, while C predicts H(U)+#D_(4) t^4 with #D_(4)=mu_X mu_Y: the cross terms are extra — sigma_X+sigma_Y is NOT generic (consistent with the toric finding: extra classes t^2 H^*(O_tau) on codim-2 strata where the residue pairing is nondegenerate). So hypothesis (GEN) is necessary in Conjecture C; products are the standard counterexample class [P].
