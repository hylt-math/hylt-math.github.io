{\LARGE\bfseries\sffamily Cohomology of Generalized Holomorphic Line Bundles\\[3pt]and Kodaira-type Theorems (Part III)}\\[8pt]
{\large Non-existence of embeddings by generalized holomorphic sections, the leaf-space Kodaira theorem,\\the Poisson--Kodaira conjecture, and non-reduced boundary}\\[12pt]
{\large Research and writing: Claude\ ;\ Problem posing: Mori}\\[3pt]
22 November 2026 (v1)\\[3pt]
{\small (Personal research note; not peer reviewed, not a preprint. Continuation of Parts I and II.\\
Unverified points are marked \tQ\ in the text.)}
\end{center}
\begin{abstract}\noindent
We consider the remaining one of the three treasures, Kodaira's embedding theorem. (1) The naive formulation, mapping to projective space by generalized holomorphic (GH) sections, fails: if $\sigma$ is non-degenerate on a dense open set then $\dim\HH^0(L)\le1$ (Proposition~\ref{prop:one}), and in regular type $k<n$ GH sections are constant along leaves, so the map is never an immersion (Proposition~\ref{prop:leaf}); we checked this for the Poisson cubic on $\mathbb{CP}^2$ and the $(2,2)$-curve on $\mathbb P^1\times\mathbb P^1$. (2) In regular type with projective leaf space the map is the composition of the projection with the classical Kodaira embedding of the leaf space (Theorem~\ref{thm:K1}); this is a direct reduction that we did not find in the literature. (3) We propose a new formulation, Poisson--Kodaira (a Poisson embedding of the pair $(X,\sigma)$ into $\mathbb P^N$): proved for torus-invariant $\sigma$ on toric varieties (Theorem~\ref{thm:PKtoric}), verified numerically for four cubics on $\mathbb P^2$ at $m=2$, and conjectural in general (Conjecture~\ref{conj:PK}). (4) We organize results on non-reduced boundary, the local formula for quasi-homogeneous singularities (including a correction), and the K\"unneth formula for products. No Kodaira-type embedding result for general generalized complex structures was found.
\end{abstract}
